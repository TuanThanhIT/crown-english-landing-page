## 4. Cac khoa hoc / dich vu nen co tren website

### IELTS

Dinh vi:

- Lo trinh thiet ke cho hoc vien Viet Nam.
- Tinh gon, thuc chien, bam sat muc tieu dau ra.
- Hoc vien duoc feedback truc tiep tren lop.
- Co homework plan ca nhan hoa.
- Tap trung vao cac ky nang Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking.

Noi dung nen viet:

- Phu hop voi hoc vien bat dau xay nen, mat goc hoac can tang band.
- Nhan manh sua Writing/Speaking chi tiet, feedback kip thoi, chien luoc lam bai ro rang.
- Co the tach thanh IELTS Foundation va IELTS Intensive nhu website hien tai dang lam.

### Giao tiep phan xa

Dinh vi:

- Giup hoc vien phan xa nhanh va tu tin dung tieng Anh trong cong viec, hoc tap va doi song.
- Lop hoc nen dua vao tinh huong that: phong van, thuyet trinh, thao luan nhom, giao tiep hang ngay.

Noi dung nen viet:

- Giam ngai noi.
- Tang kha nang phan ung tu nhien.
- Cai thien phat am, tu vung tinh huong va do troi chay khi noi.

### Business English

Website ca nhan cua chi Khanh co nhac den mang Business. Website hien tai cua Crown chua tach rieng khoa nay. Co the can nhac them vao roadmap noi dung neu trung tam that su dang day.

Noi dung co the khai thac:

- Tieng Anh cho phong van, email, hop, thuyet trinh, trao doi voi doi tac.
- Phu hop voi sinh vien sap di lam va nguoi di lam.

### Kem 1:1 ca nhan hoa

Dinh vi:

- Day theo nang luc, lich hoc va muc tieu rieng cua tung hoc vien.
- Phu hop voi hoc vien can tang toc, lich hoc linh hoat hoac can tap trung vao diem yeu cu the.

Noi dung nen viet:

- Test dau vao.
- Chan doan diem manh/diem yeu.
- Lap lo trinh rieng.
- Theo doi tien bo va dieu chinh bai tap hang tuan.

### TOEIC

Link bio cua IELTS & Giao Tiep Crown co nhac den lop TOEIC. Website hien tai chua co khoa TOEIC. Nen hoi lai chi Khanh xem TOEIC co phai khoa dang mo thuong xuyen khong. Neu co, co the them thanh khoa rieng hoac gan vao nhom "Luyen chung chi".

## 12. Phan tich lai navbar sau khi co lo trinh va cam ket

Tai thoi diem hien tai, navbar trong source dang co 7 muc cap 1:

- Trang chu
- Khoa hoc
- Lo trinh
- Cam ket
- Hoc vien
- Giang vien
- Lien he

Voi mot landing page cho trung tam tieng Anh, 7 muc cap 1 la hoi nhieu, dac biet vi "Khoa hoc" da co dropdown ben trong. Nguoi dung chinh se quan tam nhanh den: hoc khoa nao, lo trinh ra sao, co cam ket gi khong, ai day, dang ky nhu the nao. Vi vay nen giam so muc tren navbar va dua cac noi dung phu thanh section trong trang chu.

### De xuat navbar gon nhat

Nen giu 5 muc:

1. Trang chu
2. Khoa hoc
3. Lo trinh
4. Cam ket
5. Lien he

Ly do:

- "Khoa hoc" la muc quan trong nhat, nen giu dropdown.
- "Lo trinh" nen giu vi trung tam co lo trinh ro cho IELTS, Giao tiep va TOEIC.
- "Cam ket" nen giu vi day la diem ban hang manh, da co hinh rieng cho IELTS va Giao tiep.
- "Lien he" nen giu nhu CTA chinh.
- "Hoc vien" va "Giang vien" co the gop vao trang chu hoac dat trong footer, khong can nam tren navbar cap 1.

### Dropdown Khoa hoc nen gom nhung gi

Nen doi dropdown tu 4 muc hien tai thanh nhom khoa hoc that su theo brochure/link bio:

- IELTS
- Giao tiep phan xa
- TOEIC
- Kem 1:1

Neu van muon chi tiet IELTS, co the de trong trang IELTS thay vi tren dropdown:

- Foundation - 3.5+
- Newbie - 5.0+
- Advance - 6.0+

Ly do: neu dua Foundation/Newbie/Advance ra dropdown ngay, navbar se bi dai va nguoi moi vao website chua chac hieu minh thuoc level nao. Tot hon la cho ho bam vao "IELTS", trong trang do co test dau vao/lo trinh.

### Gop cac trang hien tai nhu the nao

- "Giang vien": nen bien thanh section "Founder / Doi ngu" tren trang chu, va co the co link footer neu can SEO.
- "Hoc vien": nen bien thanh section "Feedback hoc vien" tren trang chu. Chi tach thanh trang rieng khi co nhieu anh feedback, bang diem, cau chuyen hoc vien.
- "Lo trinh": co the giu thanh trang rieng neu co day du noi dung cho IELTS, Giao tiep, TOEIC. Neu noi dung con ngan, nen scroll anchor tren trang chu.
- "Cam ket": nen giu vi hinh anh cam ket la trust signal manh. Trang nay nen co 2 nhom ro: Cam ket IELTS va Cam ket Giao tiep.
